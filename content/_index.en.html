Five continents, connected to explore the deepest universe in the near-infrared.

SUPER-IRNET-2.0 builds on the achievements of SUPER-IRNET (2021–2025) to create an international hub that strategically connects research in wide-field near-infrared surveys around the world. By coordinating ground- and space-based observatories, we are building the foundation for Japan and a new generation of researchers to lead near-infrared astronomy.

Our mission

01

Connect ground and space

We coordinate observations by Euclid and Roman with ULTIMATE-Subaru, TAO/SWIMS, and PRIME to advance wide and deep near-infrared surveys.

02

Connect five continents

Our multilateral, multi-wavelength network brings together researchers in Japan, the USA, France, the UK, Australia, Taiwan, Korea, Chile, and South Africa.

03

Connect to the future

Early-career researchers lead collaborations, meetings, and international exchanges, preparing them to shape 2030s missions including JASMINE, GREX-PLUS, and PRIMA.

Five key projects

We link ground- and space-based facilities with complementary strengths through the shared goal of wide-field near-infrared observation.

EuclidESA's space telescope mapping the vast universe in the near-infrared
RomanNASA's high-sensitivity, high-resolution wide-field space telescope
ULTIMATE-SubaruA new adaptive optics system transforming Subaru's wide-field near-infrared capability
SWIMS / TAOA versatile near-infrared instrument for the TAO 6.5 m telescope in Chile
PRIMEA telescope carrying out wide-field infrared surveys from South Africa
