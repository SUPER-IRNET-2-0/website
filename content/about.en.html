About SUPER-IRNET-2.0

SUPER-IRNET-2.0 is a JSPS Core-to-Core Program that builds an international research network for the era of deep and wide near-infrared surveys of the universe with space- and ground-based telescopes.

The program aims to strategically connect worldwide research activities related to wide-field near-infrared exploration and to create a path for Japan, especially early-career researchers, to lead this field internationally. It promotes exchanges among researchers working with Subaru Telescope next-generation instruments, ESA's Euclid mission, NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, and related wide-field near-infrared projects.

By combining leading space telescopes with powerful ground-based near-infrared instruments in Japan, SUPER-IRNET-2.0 seeks to open new observational windows into the early universe. Key areas include coordinated observations between Euclid, Roman, and Subaru; the realization of ULTIMATE-Subaru, the next-generation wide-field ground-layer adaptive optics system; and pioneering science with university-led facilities such as PRIME and TAO/SWIMS.
